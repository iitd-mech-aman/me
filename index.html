Hi, I'm Aman.

My curiosity about mechanical engineering began in high school, when I once took apart my bicycle and couldn't quite put it back together. What started as a small mishap left me curious about how things are designed, how their parts work together, and what makes a mechanism function.

At IIT Delhi, that curiosity has grown beyond the things I can see and assemble. Courses in fluid mechanics, thermodynamics, solid mechanics, and kinematics have introduced me to different ways of understanding the physical world. Each has opened up new questions I want to explore.

I'm now looking for an opportunity to take that curiosity beyond coursework and into research—to learn how engineers investigate problems, test ideas, and develop a deeper understanding of how things work.

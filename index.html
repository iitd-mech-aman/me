Background

What I've worked on

I'm pursuing a B.Tech. in Mechanical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi. My coursework has introduced me to subjects including engineering mechanics, solid mechanics, thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, kinematics and dynamics of machines, and introductory programming.

Outside coursework, I've worked on CAD models and practical builds, including a coaxial swerve-drive mechanism, a line-follower robot, Arduino- and ESP32-based projects, and an automated winch lift mechanism. These projects have given me initial exposure to mechanical design, basic electronics, coding, and making systems work in practice.

I'm still early in my engineering journey. My projects have been opportunities to explore and try things out rather than substantial research work. I'm continuing to build my foundations and learn what it takes to investigate engineering problems more deeply.
